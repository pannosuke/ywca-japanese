# Roadmap

0.5.0 replaces the game. The installed app is review, quiz, conjugation, reading, and stats.

## Now

- Review through AnkiDroid, with the saved deck and FSRS when Anki is unavailable.
- Short quizzes from the deck, worksheets, graded homework, and class quizzes.
- Conjugation drills for the forms class has reached, biased toward misses.
- Graded readers at level 0 and 1.
- Stats, and a progress file Eric can send back.
- Content added as JSON after class, without a new APK.

## Next

Refinement of those five, still from class only.

- More forms as they are taught. A form the app can already grade is turned on in `forms.json`. A new pattern needs a small app update.
- More readers aimed at the misses in the progress file.
- Homework and class-quiz scores as they come in.
- Worksheet answer keys for sheets he wants to grade himself.

## Later

- Speech.
- Shared packs.
- Photograph grading stays with the person adding content, not in the app.

The deckbuilder, the map, the yokai, rarity, gems, bosses, and booster packs are not coming back.
